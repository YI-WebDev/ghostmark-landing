Overview

What the license unlocks

How it works

Three stages to reroute

Encrypt on save

Security

No exaggeration. Be explicit about what it does and does not protect.

What NinjaMark does protect

  • Local encryption for bookmark URLs and titles
  • Blocking protected URLs in normal tabs for bookmarks and watchlist domains
  • Automatic history cleanup and tracker parameter removal when a tab is blocked
  • Routing protected pages into incognito only after authentication
  • Clearing unlocked state when the session ends

What NinjaMark does not claim

  • Perfect secrecy for all metadata such as folder names
  • Hiding DNS activity or network logs from the OS or router
  • Complete invisibility beyond the limits of Chrome APIs
  • Replacing a VPN or anonymity service

FAQ

Common questions before purchase

What is sold as NinjaMark Premium?

It is a digital license that unlocks Premium features inside the NinjaMark Chrome extension. After purchase, you receive a license key and activate it in the extension.

Is bookmark data sent to a server?

No. Protected bookmark data is designed to stay local, and NinjaMark does not send that data to its own servers.

What gets encrypted?

Bookmark URLs and titles are encrypted locally. Folder names remain plaintext so they can still be listed in the UI.

Do I need Premium to use the extension?

No. Core encrypted storage, normal-tab blocking, incognito reopen after authentication, and session controls are available on the free plan.

Which features are Premium-only?

Per-folder passwords, the panic shortcut, duress mode with an emergency password, and encrypted backup export/import are Premium features.

Which permissions or settings are required?

The tabs permission is optional. When granted, it enables automatic blocking by tab monitoring and quick-add from the current tab. To open protected URLs in incognito, Chrome's “Allow in Incognito” setting must also be enabled.

What is the watchlist?

The watchlist lets you register domains without saving a bookmark. Access to those domains is blocked through the same lock-screen flow as protected bookmarks.
